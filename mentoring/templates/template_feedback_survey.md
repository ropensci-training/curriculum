# rOpenSci Champions Program Meeting Feedback

We ask you for a quick anonymous feedback on the meeting in order to improve them.

## Which meeting are you providing feedback? \*

You can use the name of the meeting

## What did you like the most? \*

(e.g., something we have to maintain, a positive thing we did)

## What did you like the least? \*

(e.g., something you would like to see changed or improved)

## Can you rate this meeting? \*

In a scale from 1-worst to 5-best.

All the questions mark with \* are mandatory.
